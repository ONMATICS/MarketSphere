Go to My Orders and open the order you'd like to track. You'll see the current status, courier details, and tracking number if one is available.

You can cancel an order as long as it hasn't been shipped yet. Open the order from My Orders and select Cancel Order. Once shipped, you'll need to request a return instead.

Most items can be returned within the seller's stated return window. Start a return from the order details page, choose a reason, and follow the prompts to arrange pickup or drop-off.

Refunds are typically processed within 3–7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
